About Us

Founded in 2015, FitTime was built on the belief that everyone deseves a welcoming place to improve their health.Our mission is to empower our members throughfitness, knowledge, and a suppotive community.create a gym where everyone feels welcome, regardless of their fitness level. Armed with just five pieces of equipment and a whole lot of passion, we started with 50 founding members who believed in our mission. Today, that small community has grown into three thriving locations serving over 4,000 members. But our core philosophy remains unchanged: fitness should empower, not intimidate. We've never aimed to be the biggest gym in town—just the most supportive. For nine years, we've been proving that real transformation happens when people feel seen, supported, and celebrated every step of the way
